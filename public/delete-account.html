Request Account & Data Deletion

At Fipmoney, we respect your right to privacy and ownership of your personal information. In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations, this page outlines how you can permanently delete your Fipmoney account and associated data.

App Name: Fipmoney • Developer: Fipmoney Technologies Private Limited • Package ID: com.fipmoney.app

Steps to Request Account Deletion

You can submit an account deletion request either directly from inside the Fipmoney mobile app or via our web request form below without needing to keep the app installed.

📱 Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the Fipmoney App

1

Open App & Sign In

Open the Fipmoney app on your Android device and log in with your registered mobile number.

2

Go to Settings

Tap your profile icon or navigate to the Settings tab from the main navigation menu.

3

Select "Security & Account"

Scroll down to the Account Management section and tap Delete Account & Data.

4

Verify via OTP & Confirm

Review the deletion summary, enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number, and confirm permanent closure.

🌐 Method 2: Submit Web Request (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or do not have access to your mobile device, submit the form below. Our support team will verify your identity via SMS/email and process the deletion within 7 business days.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

Google Play requires apps to clearly specify the types of data that are permanently deleted, what is retained, and the statutory legal retention periods.

Data Category Specific Data Types Status Retention Period
Account & Identity Full name, profile photo, phone number mapping, email address, user code Permanently Deleted Purged within 7 days of confirmation
Authentication & Security Login sessions, biometric verification tokens, device binding hashes, passwords Permanently Deleted Immediate revocation upon request
Device & App Usage Device IP addresses, OS version, advertising IDs, app analytics, push tokens Permanently Deleted Purged within 7 days
Marketing Preferences Promotional SMS/email opt-ins, referral tracking links, notification preferences Permanently Deleted Immediate
Financial Transaction Records Purchase/sale invoices, payment gateway reference IDs (UPI/Bank UTR), metal ledger records Legally Retained 5 Years as mandated by Section 12 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002 and Income Tax Act, 1961.
KYC Audit Proofs Aadhaar/PAN verification audit logs, compliance officer approval logs Legally Retained 5 Years in secure, read-only cold storage for statutory audit compliance only.
